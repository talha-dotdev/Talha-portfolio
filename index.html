Inside Klyvero

Real screens from the running application, shown exactly as they appear. Select any screen to enlarge it.

Dashboard

Business dashboard

See what's happening in your business at a glance.

  • Today's sales and purchases, inventory value, cash received, receivables, and payables
  • Seven-day sales vs purchases trend and top inventory items by quantity
  • Quick actions: New Sale, New Purchase, Add Customer, Add Supplier, Record Payment, Inventory Adjustment
  • An alerts area for low stock, payment due, and balance alerts

Business operations

Sales management

Record and manage sales while keeping customers, products, invoices, payments, and inventory connected.

  • A sales dashboard with today's, monthly, and all-time sales, plus outstanding balance and returns
  • Payment status breakdown: unpaid, partially paid, paid, overdue, and returned
  • Top customers and top-selling items, with separate Orders and Returns views

Purchase management

Manage purchases from suppliers and keep purchasing records connected to inventory and financial information.

  • Search by purchase number, supplier, or notes, and filter by payment status and date range
  • Status tracking from Draft and Ordered through Partial, Received, Completed, or Cancelled
  • Total, amount paid, and remaining balance shown for every purchase, with a Returns view

Inventory

Inventory and warehouses

Keep track of stock across your business and maintain a clear view of what is available.

  • Separate views for Overview, Items, Stock, Warehouses, Movements, and Transfers
  • Stock by warehouse: quantity, reserved, available, reorder level, and value
  • In-stock, low-stock, and out-of-stock status with a stock health indicator
  • Add opening stock and search by item name or code

Reporting

Business reports

Turn operational data into useful business information through reports and summaries.

  • A business overview of sales, purchases, returns, receivables, payables, and cash movement
  • Report views for Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Employees, Khata, Payments, Returns & Adjustments, and Statements
  • Choose a period, then Print or export to CSV, Excel, or PDF

Administration

Security & authentication

Sign-in protection that each user can manage from inside the application.

  • Two-factor authentication with an email code or a connected authenticator app
  • Passkey sign-in with Windows Hello, fingerprint, face recognition, or a security key
  • Security questions as a backup way to verify identity
  • Active sessions with sign-out from other devices, and a login history of recent attempts
  • JWT-based authentication with role-based permissions behind it

Business settings

Company details, branding, and defaults for the whole organization.

  • Company profile with logo, company name, and business type, used on report letterheads and printed receipts and statements
  • Regional settings for country, currency, and timezone, controlling how money and dates are displayed
  • Separate settings for Financial, Sales, Purchases, Khata, Inventory, Employees, and Printing & Documents

Users & permissions

Control who can access the system and what each user is allowed to do, through user accounts, roles, and permissions.

Backup & data protection

Database backup and restore for the business's local data.

Installs like a normal Windows application

Klyvero is packaged as a Windows application, so businesses can install and run the system as a normal desktop application. The application manages its local PostgreSQL database as part of the installation architecture.
